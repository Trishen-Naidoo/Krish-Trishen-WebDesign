About The Festival

One campus transformed for
minds that perform, build, and explore.

Most festivals divide the night into performers on a stage and spectators in the dark. ONE NIGHT dissolves that boundary completely across VIT Mauritius.

From the moment lanterns ignite on Quantum Lawn at 6:00 PM until the acoustic sunrise finale at 6:30 AM, every student carries a Glow-Band and enters the pact: no audience, only participants.

Explore the Night

Dusk × Dawn

6:00 PM • Quantum Lawn

The Lantern Ceremony

Everyone decorates and lights a lantern together.

Sign your Zero-Spectator Pact onto biodegradable paper lanterns. At 6:30 PM campus floodlights dim and 5,000 lanterns lift the evening together.

Unlocks: Initial Glow-Band Spark
7:30 PM • All 6 Faculty Zones

The Zones Open

Collect stamps in each zone to fill your glow-band.

All six themed zones unlock simultaneously. Participate in one challenge or collaboration per zone to earn all six stamps on your digital wristband.

6 Stamps = Full-Spectrum Pass
12:00 AM • 360° Central Platform

The Reveal

Campus lights return, secret headliner takes the stage.

Following a synchronized countdown, campus architectural lights flash back on and our unannounced headliner performs in the round with student instrumentalists.

Synchronized Wristband Illumination
1:00 AM • Fine Arts Amphitheatre

The Open Stage

Any student gets 3 minutes on the mic.

No auditions, no gatekeeping. Tap your Glow-Band at the stage booth for 180 seconds of live music, poetry, stand-up, or improvised synth sets.

Live House Backing Band Available
5:30 AM – 6:30 AM • Quantum Lawn East

Sunrise Chill

Acoustic music and hot drinks on the lawn until dawn.

Unwind with spiced chai, espresso, blankets, and acoustic guitars as first light hits VIT Mauritius Campus—closing with the 6:30 AM sunrise gathering.

Sunrise Breakfast for Full Glow-Bands
